WAGS · Support Widget

However you'd like to give, there's a button for it.

A floating "Give" button wired to unified-modal-v2_8_0.js — donation methods, a QR-pay image, social links, and legal docs, each routed through the same shared modal shell.

Live demo

Tap the gold button in the bottom-right corner to open the support menu, then try each option.

Legal, Privacy Notice, FAQ, Trouble donating? and Other ways to give are wired to your real Google Drive links. More Ways, QR Pay, and Social use placeholder values — see the TODO comments at the top of the script for exactly what to replace.
New in v2.8.0

The support button above opens a hand-built flyout that then triggers UnifiedModal.open() dialogs. This button instead calls the engine's new openMenu() directly — an anchored popup next to the button itself, no backdrop, no page dimming. Its last item still opens the same "Social" dialog as the FAB above, on top of the popup, to show they share one Escape/stack.
